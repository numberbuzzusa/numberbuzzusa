What Time Is the Powerball Drawing? Days, Times & Where to Watch

Lottery Guide · NumberBuzz USA

The Powerball drawing is held three nights a week — every Monday, Wednesday and Saturday at 10:59 p.m. Eastern Time — live from the Florida Lottery draw studio in Tallahassee. That single Eastern start time means the clock on your wall reads differently depending on where you live: 9:59 p.m. Central, 8:59 p.m. Mountain and 7:59 p.m. Pacific. Ticket sales cut off one to two hours before the draw in most states, so buy earlier in the evening to be safe.

Arizona does not observe daylight saving time, so during the summer months Arizona lines up with Pacific Time (7:59 p.m.) and in winter with Mountain Time (8:59 p.m.). Hawaii players see the draw at 5:59 p.m. in winter and 4:59 p.m. in summer, and Alaska at 6:59 p.m. (5:59 p.m. in summer). The Eastern start time itself never changes — 10:59 p.m. ET all year round.

When do ticket sales close?

Each state sets its own sales cut-off, and it is earlier than most people expect: typically one to two hours before the drawing. New York stops at 10:00 p.m. ET on draw nights, for example, while some states close as early as 9:00 p.m. local time. If you buy through a state lottery app or website, the app will simply refuse the sale once the cut-off passes and roll your entry into the next draw. The practical rule: buy before dinner on draw day and you will never miss out.

Where to watch the drawing

The drawing is broadcast live on television stations in many markets and streamed on Powerball’s official website and YouTube channel. Plenty of players skip the live show entirely and simply check the numbers afterwards — the NumberBuzz homepage posts the winning numbers, the jackpot and the rollover status every morning, and the live countdown there tells you exactly how long is left until the next draw. What happens right after the draw

Once the balls are drawn, officials verify the result and count ticket sales across all 46 participating states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands. That is why the official jackpot for the next draw sometimes takes an hour or two to appear: it depends on how many tickets were sold and whether anyone matched all six numbers. If nobody wins, the jackpot rolls — and if it was won, the next jackpot resets to $20 million. Wondering how Powerball’s schedule compares with the other big game? A short history of the Monday draw

For most of its life, Powerball drew only twice a week, on Wednesdays and Saturdays. The Monday drawing was added in August 2021, turning Powerball into a three-nights-a-week game and creating the modern weekly rhythm it now shares with Mega Millions. The change was made to build jackpots faster and give players a Monday-night ritual — and it worked: three draws a week means rollovers compound more quickly, and billion-dollar jackpots, once unthinkable, have become a recurring event.

What if a draw is delayed?

Very rarely, a drawing is delayed — a sales-data discrepancy or a technical verification issue can push the draw past its scheduled time, occasionally by hours. When that happens, the draw still takes place under audit supervision once the problem is cleared, and the numbers drawn are the official ones. If results look late on a draw night, do not trust social-media posts of “the numbers”; wait for the verified result, which NumberBuzz posts the following morning.

One last timing habit worth forming: results are not official the second the balls drop. State lotteries reconcile sales before confirming the jackpot outcome, so give it a little time, then check your ticket calmly against the verified numbers — our step-by-step checking guide shows exactly how.

Frequently asked questions

What time is the Powerball drawing tonight?

If tonight is a Monday, Wednesday or Saturday, the drawing is at 10:59 p.m. Eastern — 9:59 p.m. Central, 8:59 p.m. Mountain, 7:59 p.m. Pacific. On other nights there is no Powerball draw; Mega Millions plays Tuesdays and Fridays instead.

Why 10:59 p.m. and not 11:00?

The odd minute is deliberate: it lets local TV stations slot the live drawing just before their late news, and it gives every state a clean cut-off for final sales reconciliation.

How soon are results official?

Unofficial numbers appear within minutes. The verified result and next jackpot are usually confirmed within a couple of hours. Always confirm a winning ticket with the official lottery before claiming.
