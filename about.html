About Us

NumberBuzz USA — the numbers America checks every day.

NumberBuzz USA is a daily-check hub built for one simple habit: the numbers millions of Americans look up every single morning, gathered on one fast, readable page. Instead of opening a dozen different sites, you can check everything here in under a minute.

What you will find here

  • Lottery results — the latest Powerball and Mega Millions winning numbers, the next jackpots, live draw countdowns, hot & cold number statistics, ticket checkers and a jackpot tax calculator.
  • Precious metals — indicative gold and silver spot prices per troy ounce.
  • Gas prices — the AAA national average plus regular gasoline averages for all 50 states.
  • Market numbers — the Dow, S&P 500 and Nasdaq closes, Bitcoin and Ethereum prices, and average US mortgage rates.
  • Deals of the day — standout discounts checked each morning.
  • Weather & sports — tomorrow’s forecast highs for major US cities and last night’s NBA and NHL finals.
  • USA Jobs Today — around 20 fresh job openings across warehouse, driving, healthcare, customer service, remote and federal work.
  • Daily fun — today’s Wordle answer (hidden behind hints, so no accidental spoilers) and a daily horoscope for all 12 signs.

Who runs NumberBuzz USA

NumberBuzz USA is owned and operated by Shahiryar Munir, an independent publisher. The site is a small, independent project — not a news corporation and not affiliated with Powerball, Mega Millions, AAA, or any lottery, employer or retailer mentioned on the page.

How the page is updated

The page is refreshed every morning with a new dated snapshot. Every figure carries its date, and the numbers are compiled from publicly available sources — official lottery results, AAA fuel data, market reports and public job boards — and cross-checked at build time before publishing. Because this is a snapshot rather than a live feed, prices and jackpots can move after publication; the date printed at the top of the page always tells you exactly how fresh the information is.

Our promise is simple: real numbers, clear dates, no invented figures — and if you ever spot something that looks wrong, please tell us via the Contact page so we can fix it in the next refresh.
